Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version: If you sign in, we keep your account details and what you put into College Hub in our database, so you can use it on any device. If you continue without an account, your data stays in your own browser. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you. You can download your data or delete your account at any time.

Who we are

This policy applies to College Hub ("College Hub", "the app", "we", "us"), the web app at https://laxed-college-hub.vercel.app. College Hub is a free, open-source student dashboard for class schedules, deadlines, notes and grades. It is an independent student project. The source code is public at github.com/Laxus-CMD/Laxed-College-Hub. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Using College Hub without an account

If you choose Continue without an account, your courses, schedule, tasks, notes, grades, display name and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see, recover or delete them. If you later sign in, College Hub asks before adding them to your account.

Information we collect from Google

College Hub offers Sign in with Google through Google Identity Services. It requests only these Google OAuth scopes: openid, email and profile. If you choose Continue with Google and give permission, Google shares the following with the app:

  • Name (full name and first name)
  • Email address, and whether Google has verified it
  • Profile picture (a link to the photo on Google's servers)
  • Google Account ID, a number that identifies your account

We do not receive your Google password. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts or any other Google data, and College Hub cannot read them.

How we use your Google information

College Hub uses your Google information only to:

  • create your College Hub account, sign you in and keep you signed in
  • make sure only you can reach your account and the data in it
  • show your name, email and photo in the app so you can see which account you are using
  • fill in your first name in the greeting (you can change it in Your data)
  • let administrators manage accounts and stop abuse

It is not used for any other purpose.

College Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not:

  • sell it
  • use it for advertising, or to build profiles for anyone else
  • use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • share it, except with the service providers listed below that run College Hub for us, or if the law requires it

Other information we keep for accounts

  • What you enter: your courses and class schedule, professors' names, emails and office hours if you add them, tasks, imported calendar deadlines, notes, grades, GPA settings and display name. Imported .ics files are read inside your browser. Only the deadlines you choose are saved.
  • Sign-in records: when you sign in, your IP address, and your browser type. We use these to keep accounts secure.
  • Password, for accounts that use one: only a scrambled form (a salted hash) that cannot be turned back into your password.

We use what you enter only to show it back to you on your devices. We use the rest to run College Hub and keep it secure.

Who can see it

Other students cannot see your account or your data. Administrators can see your name, email, role and sign-in activity, but the app does not show them your schedule, tasks, notes or grades. The people who run College Hub have technical access to the database. They look at what you have entered only when it is needed to keep the service working or secure, to help with a problem you report, or to follow the law.

Service providers

  • Neon hosts the database that holds accounts and their data. See the Neon Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts the website and its server. Like any web host, Vercel may log technical details such as your IP address and browser type. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google Identity Services. The sign-in button is loaded from Google, and Google handles the sign-in itself. Your profile picture is loaded from Google's servers. Google may set its own cookies for this. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub stores backups of the database in a private place. They are encrypted before they get there. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

These providers process data for us and may do so outside the Philippines. We do not share, sell, rent or give your information to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

When you sign in, College Hub sets one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for accounts to work and is not used for tracking. Your browser also remembers your theme, and the account details shown in the app, until you sign out. While you are signed in, your schedule, tasks, notes and grades are not stored in the browser, with one exception: changes you just made that have not reached your account yet, for example because you are offline, are kept in the browser until they do. They are deleted as soon as your account has them, when you choose to sign out without saving them, or when you delete them from the sign-in page. College Hub has no analytics, tracking pixels or ads.

How long we keep your data

  • Your account and what you enter: until you delete it.
  • Your sign-in on a device: until you sign out, up to 7 days.
  • Changes not yet saved to your account: in the browser, until your account has them or you delete them.
  • The security log (sign-ins and account changes, with IP addresses): about 90 days.
  • Backups: up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct what you entered in the app at any time.
  • Download a copy with Your data, then Download my data.
  • Delete what you entered with Clear all data, or delete your account and everything in it with Delete account. It leaves our database right away, and leaves backups within 30 days. Security log entries stay until they expire, without your name, email or account.
  • Remove College Hub's access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. This does not delete your College Hub account.
  • If you are in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 gives you rights over your personal data, including to be informed, to access, correct, object, erase or block, and to get a copy. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Sign-in cookies cannot be read by scripts, every request is checked to belong to the signed-in account, and backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires. Sign out when you use a shared computer.

Children

College Hub is made for college students. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Past versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Laxus-CMD/Laxed-College-Hub/issues. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
