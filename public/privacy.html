Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version: College Hub has no server. Everything you enter, and the basic profile Google shares when you sign in, is saved only in your own browser. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

This policy applies to College Hub ("College Hub", "the app", "we", "us"), the web app at https://laxed-college-hub.vercel.app. College Hub is a free, open-source student dashboard for class schedules, deadlines, notes and grades. The source code is public at github.com/Laxus-CMD/Laxed-College-Hub. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect from Google

College Hub offers Sign in with Google through Google Identity Services. It requests only these Google OAuth scopes: openid, email and profile. If you choose Continue with Google and give permission, Google shares the following with the app:

  • Name (full name and first name)
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (a link to the photo on Google's servers)
  • Google Account ID, a number that identifies your account

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts or any other Google data, and College Hub cannot read them.

How we use your Google information

College Hub uses your Google information only to:

  • sign you in and remember that you are signed in on this browser
  • show your name, email and photo in the app so you can see which account you are using
  • fill in your first name in the greeting (you can change it in Your data)

It is not used for any other purpose.

College Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not:

  • send your Google information to any server we run (we have none)
  • sell it, or share it with anyone
  • use it for advertising, or to build profiles for anyone else
  • use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • let any person read it

You can use College Hub without a Google Account by choosing Continue without an account.

How we share your information

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your Google information or anything you enter in the app to any third party. College Hub has no server or database, so your information is never sent to us. The only outside services involved are the ones listed under Third-party services below.

Information you enter

Your courses, class schedule, tasks, imported Canvas (.ics) deadlines, notes, grades, display name and appearance setting are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. They are never uploaded. Imported .ics files are read inside your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Where your data is stored

On your device only. Because there is no account database, your data does not follow you to another browser or device, and we cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

Third-party services

  • Google Identity Services. The sign-in button is loaded from Google, and Google handles the sign-in itself. Your profile picture is loaded from Google's servers. Google may set its own cookies for this. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel. The website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may log technical details such as your IP address and browser type when you load the site. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

College Hub has no analytics, tracking pixels or ads, and sets no cookies of its own.

How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

Your Google profile and app data stay in your browser until you remove them. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side.

  • Sign out (in Your data) removes your Google profile from your browser.
  • Clear all data (in Your data) removes your courses, tasks, notes and grades.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything College Hub has saved.
  • You can remove College Hub's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Your data is protected by your browser and device, so anyone with access to your browser profile can see it. Sign out and clear your data on shared computers.

Children

College Hub is made for college students. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Past versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Laxus-CMD/Laxed-College-Hub/issues.
